# Lion run cycle (test extraction)

- Source: "Chasing Lion Green Screen Video" by the YouTube channel "Green Screen Wiki", https://youtu.be/t5fKzFv87aU (1920×1080, 29 fps).
- Terms in the video description: "free chromakey footage. Don't re upload as it is with green screen in background." There is no formal license. Confirm with the creator before shipping in a public or commercial release.
- Extracted: source frames 200–210, one complete stride of 11 drawings (~0.36 s at 29 fps). Chroma-keyed, despilled and mirrored to face left (the race runs right to left). Every frame is cropped to the same 1336×660 box, so the frames stay aligned.
- `lion-run-preview.gif` is a looping preview over a placeholder ground.
